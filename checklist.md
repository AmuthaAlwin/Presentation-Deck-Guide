# Pre-Presentation Checklist

Run through this before you present any technical deck.

## Argument

- [ ] Does every slide support the argument from your opening, or is it just "interesting"?
- [ ] Could someone who missed the talk understand your ask from the last slide alone?
- [ ] Have you cut the exploratory content you needed for your own thinking but the audience doesn't?

## Diagrams

- [ ] Can you explain the core diagram without reading the labels off the slide?
- [ ] Does each diagram cover exactly one concept, not three combined?
- [ ] Are the arrows labeled, not just the boxes?

## Delivery

- [ ] Have you timed the talk against the actual slot length, not your estimate?
- [ ] Do you know which two or three slides you'd cut first if you're running long?
- [ ] Is there a single slide an audience member could screenshot and still get the point?

## Close

- [ ] Does the last slide say exactly what you want the audience to do next?
- [ ] Is there a way to follow up (link, contact, repo) on the final slide?
