# Slide Outline Template

Fill this in before opening a slide tool. Keep each section to two or three sentences, if you can't, the argument probably isn't tight enough yet.

## 1. The problem

What problem exists, stated in terms the audience already cares about?

> _Your answer here_

## 2. The constraint

What tradeoff or constraint makes this problem hard to solve well?

> _Your answer here_

## 3. The approach

What are you proposing, and why this approach over the obvious alternatives?

> _Your answer here_

## 4. The evidence

What shows this actually works? Benchmarks, a demo, a case study, metrics.

> _Your answer here_

## 5. The ask

What do you want from the audience: a decision, feedback, adoption, funding?

> _Your answer here_

---

Once this is filled in, map each section to one or more slides. Most sections need between one and four slides. If a section needs more than five, it's probably two arguments, not one.
